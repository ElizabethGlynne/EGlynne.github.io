Teaching Philosophy

I approach biology education as an opportunity to help students understand the living world as a collection of interconnected, dynamic systems. Rather than emphasizing memorization of isolated facts, I encourage students to investigate how biological systems work, persist, and change across time and levels of organization.

Courses & Curriculum

I currently teach BIO 112 at Des Moines Area Community College (DMACC), organized around a central question: How does life work, persist, and change?

Read about my curriculum approach

Students connect foundational biological ideas to cells and energy, continuity and inheritance, molecular biology, and evolution. Laboratory investigations, model-building, authentic data, and evidence-based explanations connect processes across levels of organization.

As I develop additional courses, I aim to maintain clear objectives, meaningful expectations, opportunities for practice and feedback, and assessments aligned with what students should be able to explain and apply.

Assignments, assessments, grades, and course-specific announcements for enrolled students remain in DMACC's official learning systems.
