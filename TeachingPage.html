About My Teaching

I am an Instructor of Biology at Des Moines Area Community College. My teaching emphasizes inclusive, student-centered instruction grounded in real organisms, evolutionary thinking, and evidence-based inquiry. I want students to understand not only biological concepts, but also how scientists develop, test, and revise explanations of the living world.

I use laboratory investigations, authentic scientific data, comparative examples, and active-learning activities to help students connect biological processes across levels of organization. My approach emphasizes conceptual understanding, pattern recognition, scientific reasoning, and opportunities to practice applying ideas rather than simply memorizing terminology.

I am also interested in how scientific computing, visual models, and digital anatomy tools can make complex biological systems more accessible. I aim to create learning experiences that support different starting points while maintaining meaningful expectations for scientific understanding.

Teaching Specialties and Interests

  • Introductory Biology (majors and non-majors)
  • Organismal Biology, Zoology, and Herpetology
  • Vertebrate Anatomy and Comparative Morphology
  • Ecology- and evolution-centered biology instruction
  • Laboratory inquiry, active learning, and authentic data analysis
  • Scientific computing, computational models, and digital anatomy tools
